IKEA SKÅDIS Hair Dryer Holder with Cord Hook

## Description

A hair dryer holder for the IKEA SKÅDIS pegboard. The dryer rests on an open ring with its handle hanging through, and the power cord hangs on a side hook.

**Features**

- Open C-shaped ring, 80 mm inner diameter, with a 35 mm front opening so the handle or cord can pass through
- 2 mm rounded edges on the ring so it does not scratch the dryer
- J-shaped cord hook on the right side
- Two hooks on the back, 40 mm apart, for SKÅDIS slots
- One solid part, 96 x 103.5 x 80 mm

**Dimensions**

- Backplate: 60 x 80 x 5 mm
- Ring: 80 mm inner diameter, 8 mm wall, 20 mm tall
- SKÅDIS hooks: 4.8 mm wide, 40 mm center to center
- Cord hook: 15 mm wide, 20 mm deep

**Printing**

- Print upright, as it hangs on the board. The ring and the bottom of the backplate sit on the bed.
- Supports are required under the two back hooks and the cord hook. Tree supports work well. Leave "On build plate only" off.
- Printed on a Bambu Lab A1, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, PLA.

**Notes**

- PLA softens at around 55 to 60 °C. Let the dryer cool for a few seconds (cool shot) before hanging it, or print in PETG.
- The OpenSCAD source is included. All dimensions are variables at the top of the file, so you can change the ring diameter for your dryer.
